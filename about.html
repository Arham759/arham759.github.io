SEAM is a small, independent clothing catalog based in Pakistan. We put together pieces we'd actually want to wear — everyday basics, outerwear that holds up, and traditional wear for the pieces that matter.

What we're about

We're not chasing seasonal trends. The idea behind SEAM is simple: heavyweight cotton, real denim, and cuts that still fit right after a year of regular wear. Whether it's a plain tee or an embroidered three-piece suit, we'd rather have fewer, better pieces than a catalog that turns over every month.

Where we are

SEAM is run out of Pakistan, and prices on this site are listed in PKR. We're currently in a pre-launch phase — you can browse the full catalog and save items to your wishlist, and ordering is being set up. Check our Contact page for the fastest way to reach us in the meantime.
