Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SEAM is a small independent clothing store. This policy explains what information we collect when you browse or contact us, and how it's used.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you give us directly — such as your name, email address, or WhatsApp number, if you contact us to ask about a product or place an order.
  • Automatically collected information — like your browser type, device type, and general location (city/country level), collected through standard website analytics.
  • Cookies — small files stored in your browser to remember things like your cart or wishlist for the current session, and to support advertising shown on this site (see Section 4).

Your bag and wishlist are currently stored only in your browser for your session — they are not saved on our servers, since checkout is not yet open.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and process orders once ordering is available
  • To improve the site and understand which products and pages visitors find useful
  • To show relevant advertising through third-party ad networks

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers who help run the site (hosting, analytics, advertising)
  • Authorities, if required by law

4. Advertising and cookies

This site may display ads served by third-party advertising networks (for example, Google AdSense). These networks may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, or opt out of personalized advertising through your ad network's own settings (for Google, via Google Ads Settings).

5. Your choices

  • You can clear your browser's cookies at any time to reset your session data.
  • You can contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to request it be deleted.

6. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email us at you@example.com.
